## Description

Turn a spare iPhone into a simple portrait dog camera, with live picture and sound
on your own server.

Choose 480p, 720p or 1080p, plug in your camera phone and tap Start Camera. Use Dark
Screen after positioning the phone, then open your private browser viewer on another
phone to see the room and hear your dog.

Poochcam is for self-hosters. You need a compatible server and publishing address;
our guide provides a Linux/Docker setup using MediaMTX. The app is free, but your
server provider may charge for hosting and bandwidth. There is no Poochcam cloud
account, subscription, advertising, recording archive or outage-alert service.

Keep Poochcam in the foreground and the camera phone connected to external power.
Closing the app stops the stream. Always check picture and sound before leaving.

Poochcam is an independent project based on Moblin by Erik Moqvist. Open-source
credits and licenses are available inside the app and on poochcam.ca.

This is an iPhone camera utility for user-operated streaming servers, with a
dedicated simplified portrait interface. No account or payment is needed in the app.
An isolated review server is supplied below; it does not connect to a personal feed.

2. Paste the supplied HTTPS viewing link, then Save camera setup.
3. Allow Camera and Microphone. Choose 720p and tap Start Camera.
4. On another phone or computer, open the viewing link and enter the supplied viewer
   credentials. Tap Watch live and verify picture and sound.
5. Try Dark Screen on the camera phone; tap it to reveal controls.
6. Hold Stop for two seconds. Stop before choosing another resolution.
